Medical Billing Services — For US Healthcare Providers

Medical Billing & Revenue Cycle Services for US Practices

VORLEN provides remote medical billing and revenue cycle services to healthcare practices in the United States, working as an extension of your practice. Our focus is careful claims handling, denial follow-up, and steady accounts-receivable follow-up. Our billing team works remotely from Pakistan, serving practices across the United States.

Insurance Eligibility Verification

Coverage checked before the visit, so fewer claims come back denied later.

Claims Submission & Follow-up

Accurate, timely submission of clean claims, tracked until each claim reaches resolution.

Denial Management

Root-cause review of denied claims, corrections, and resubmission.

Payment Posting

ERA/EOB posting, reconciled against expected reimbursements.

Accounts Receivable Follow-up

Systematic follow-up on unpaid and aging claims.

End-to-End Revenue Cycle Management

The full cycle, from eligibility checks to final payment, with regular transparent reports.

How Working With Us Starts

1. Billing Review

With your authorization, we review your recent collection, denial, and AR aging reports — so any proposal is based on your actual numbers, not guesses.

2. Clear Proposal

Services and pricing are agreed before work starts: percentage of collections, fixed monthly, or per claim — depending on specialty, workload, and services.

3. Secure Onboarding

Before we access any patient information, we sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) and agree on data-security safeguards. You can start with a pilot project, then scale up.
